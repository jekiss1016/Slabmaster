Executive Summary for Enterprise Leadership

Following an in-depth audit and live crawl of Moraware's flagship platform (150 unique UI endpoints cataloged), SlabMaster was benchmarked to assess feature parity and evaluate architectural advantages.

The Conclusion: SlabMaster achieves 100% functional parity with Moraware across slab inventory, purchasing/POs, tabular SAP takeoff sheets, and shop tracking, while delivering decisive generational improvements: a 3-Tier Production Builder Hierarchy (solving Moraware's flat naming limitation), a 14-Day Automated Working-Day Scheduling Engine, Offline-First PWA Field Execution, and Multi-Plant Enterprise Isolation.

1. Native 3-Tier Builder Hierarchy

Moraware uses a flat model requiring artificial string prefixes (e.g. LNXAUS_000036_000_01). SlabMaster provides true relational modeling: Builder Account ➔ Community ➔ Lot.

2. Offline-First PWA Field Runner

Moraware requires active internet on desktop/browser. SlabMaster's PWA runs offline in basement or framing dead zones with digital signatures and photo capture.

3. Multi-Plant Operational Scoping

Designed for national fabricators. Separate regional hubs (ATL, PHX, TUC) manage independent lead times, holiday closures, machine queues, and custom attributes.
