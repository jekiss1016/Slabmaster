SlabMaster User Guide & Reference

Welcome to the official documentation for SlabMaster, the national multi-tenant enterprise solution for countertop fabrication, slab inventory management, job scheduling, and mobile field dispatch.

Architectural Foundations

SlabMaster is designed from the ground up to solve the operational hurdles faced by high-volume commercial stone fabricators:

  • 3-Tier Builder Hierarchy: Direct modeling of Account ➔ Community ➔ Lot eliminates flat job naming hacks.
  • Multi-Facility Plant Scoping: Independent facility hubs (e.g. ATL, PHX, TUC) with dedicated lead times, holiday calendars, and machine queues.
  • Offline PWA Engine: Field crews can execute inspection checklists, capture customer signatures, and snap installation photos even with zero cellular connectivity.

🏢 Accounts & Communities

▶️ Video Tutorial: Builder Hierarchy Deep-Dive on YouTube

The Accounts module manages master relationships with commercial production homebuilders, general contractors, and designers.

The 3-Tier Enterprise Model

  1. Builder Account: High-level corporate client (e.g., Lennar, Pulte, D.R. Horton) storing corporate billing terms and regional contacts.
  2. Subdivision / Community: Master subdivision under the builder (e.g., Silver Creek Ranch) containing location coordinates, superintendent contacts, and default pricing sheets.
  3. Lot / Unit: Individual residential or commercial unit with specific plan elevations and architectural configurations.

💼 Jobs & Field Dispatch

▶️ Video Tutorial: Jobs, Orders & Scheduling Engine on YouTube

Every job order in SlabMaster represents an actionable countertop installation milestone, automatically grouped by subdivision and lot.

Contractor Data Shielding

External subcontractors and field crews only see the job site address, lot number, and technical specs necessary to complete the install. Commercial margins, PO amounts, and billing records are strictly shielded.

Activity Sequencing

Jobs track four sequential milestones: Laser Template ➔ CAD / CAM Programming ➔ Shop Fabrication ➔ Field Installation.

CAD & Blueprint File Attachments

Attach DXF cut files, CNC toolpaths, island elevation drawings, and job site photos directly to any job record. Features real-time file upload, automatic size formatting (KB/MB), in-browser download links, and one-click deletion.

📅 Production Calendar & Auto-Scheduling

SlabMaster features an automated 14-day scheduling engine that sequences fabrication and installation milestones while strictly enforcing working days and regional facility shutdowns.

  • Working-Day Rules: Scheduling offsets skip Saturdays, Sundays, and official company holidays.
  • Plant Holiday Overrides: Configure regional shutdowns per operating facility (e.g., weather emergencies or machine maintenance).
  • Touch Swipe & Stacked Views: Touch gestures on mobile devices allow shop managers to swipe day-by-day or inspect hourly crew distributions.

💎 Slab Inventory & Remnant Management

▶️ Video Tutorial: Slab Inventory, Barcodes & Remnants on YouTube

Full tracking of raw stone materials, bundle allocations, warehouse rack coordinates, and remnant offcuts.

Key Features

  • Dimensional SQFT Calculation: Enter Length (in) and Width (in) to calculate total square footage instantly: SQFT = (L × W) / 144.
  • Remnant Offcut Linkage: Tag remnants with parent slab serial numbers to track yield utilization and residual cost basis.
  • A-Frame Bin Tagging: Assign slabs to specific physical warehouse racks (e.g., Rack B-04).
  • Thermal Barcode Printing (ISO/IEC 15417 Code 128): Generate 100% optically scannable Code 128 Subset B vector SVG barcodes for Zebra, Dymo, and Brother thermal printers. Built with exact bar/space ratio patterns, Modulo 103 checksum calculation, quiet zones, and thermal media print styling for standard 4"×2" and 3"×2" label stock.
  • Zero-Click Zebra & Industrial Scan Gun Integration: Plug-and-play support for Zebra, Honeywell, and Datalogic handheld scanners in HID keyboard-wedge mode. The global scanner listener detects rapid keystroke bursts (<70ms) and automatically routes the operator: scanning a slab serial navigates to Slab Inventory with an active filter, while scanning a job packet barcode navigates directly to the Job Detail record, accompanied by a floating HUD notification toast.
  • Job Packet Barcode Integration: Automatically embeds scannable Code 128 barcodes onto printable shop clipboard packets, enabling sawyers and CNC operators to scan physical paper packets to locate jobs instantly.

📦 Purchasing & Dock Receiving

▶️ Video Tutorial: Purchasing & Dock Receiving on YouTube

Streamline vendor purchase orders for raw slabs, sinks, and consumable tooling with integrated dock receiving and barcode minting.

Dock Receiving & Automated Slab Serialization

When a slab bundle arrives at the receiving bay, shop managers open the Dock Receive modal to verify supplier, material, and bundle counts.

  • Rack / Bay Assignment: Direct incoming bundles to specific warehouse locations (e.g. Dock Receiving Bay, Rack A-04 (Quartz Bundle Bay), or Rack B-02).
  • Auto-Minted Barcode Serials: Generates formatted serial numbers in the standard sequence (SLB-[PLANT]-[PO#]-[SEQ]) with instant barcode label preview.
  • Automated Slab Inventory Auto-Injection: Confirming receipt automatically mints serialized slab records, calculates square footage per slab, links allocated job IDs, marks the PO as RECEIVED, and updates active warehouse inventory without manual data entry.

🖥️ Shop Floor Kiosk View

▶️ Video Tutorial: Touch Kiosk Machine Stations on YouTube

A high-contrast, touch-optimized station monitor designed for fabrication machine operators (Sawjet, CNC, and Polish stations).

Machine Station Queues & Live Schedule Sync

  • Saw 1 (Bridge Saw): Initial straight-line perimeter and sink cutout rough cuts.
  • Saw 2 (Waterjet CNC): High-precision curve cuts, miters, and complex island cuts.
  • CNC Router: Edge profiling, polishing, and undermount sink drop shaping.
  • Polish Line: Hand finishing, backsplash chamfering, and final QC inspection.

Real-Time Calendar & Schedule Sync: When machine operators mark a station cut or polish as Complete on the kiosk touchscreen, the system automatically synchronizes with the master schedule, updating the job's corresponding shop activity (CNC Sawjet Cutting, Edge Polish, QC) to Complete in real time.

⚡ Modern RESTful API & SAP ERP Integration

▶️ Video Tutorial: Admin Settings, API & ERP Queue on YouTube

SlabMaster provides a modern JSON RESTful API designed to supersede legacy SOAP and .NET interfaces with full CRUD control and first-class External ID routing.

Key API Capabilities

  • External ID Routing: Directly query, upsert, and link Builder Accounts, Communities, Lots, Jobs, and Job Activities using your SAP ERP identifiers (WBS Elements, PO numbers) without translating internal UUIDs.
  • Two-Way Change Data Capture (CDC): Poll GET /api/v1/sync/changes?since=<timestamp> to mirror shop floor completion, field measure signoffs, and install progress back to SAP in real time.
  • Audit-Compliant Soft Cancellation: DELETE endpoints idempotently set status: "Cancelled" and isArchived: true to avoid breaking downstream foreign keys in SAP cost collector accounting.
  • Admin API Key Management: Generate scoped tokens (sm_live_...) directly in the Admin Portal under Settings ➔ API & ERP Integration.

🚀 Subscriber Onboarding Checklist & Initial Setup Guide

▶️ Master Walkthrough: Complete Subscriber Setup Video on YouTube

Comprehensive blueprint for provisioning and configuring a new fabricator or enterprise subscriber tenant from zero to live production.

Part 1: New Subscriber Information Checklist

Gather the following prerequisite operational and system parameters prior to tenant provisioning:

  • Organization & Identity: Legal company name, tenant domain (e.g. granitecraft.com), high-res transparent logo, and authentication mode (Entra ID SSO or Hybrid/Local).
  • Regional Facilities & Plants: Facility names, plant codes (e.g. ATL, PHX), physical yard addresses, timezones, and holiday closure calendars.
  • Crews & Machinery (Assignees): CNC Sawjet lines, CNC routers, digital laser templating techs, installation teams, and warranty repair trucks.
  • Activity Lifecycle Pipeline: Standard phase progression (Template ➔ CAD Match ➔ Sawjet ➔ Polish ➔ Field Install ➔ Sign-off) with durations and lead time dependencies.
  • Material & Slab Catalog: Stone types (Quartz, Granite, Porcelain), color brands (Cambria, Silestone, MSI), standard slab dimensions, and minimum remnant thresholds.
  • Builder Accounts & Job Sites: Top-level builder accounts, community subdivisions, lot numbers, and corresponding SAP/ERP External IDs (e.g. SAP-CUST-126954, SAP-COMM-LNXAUS, SAP-LOT-LNXAUS-036).
  • Form Templates & Sub-Grids: Inspection checklists, room-by-room Table Matrix sheets (e.g. ft_sap_config_sheet), and touch glass sign-off waivers.
  • ERP Integration Credentials: SAP S/4HANA or Moraware endpoints, REST API key scopes, and outbound retry destination URLs.

Part 2: 10-Step Chronological Setup Sequence

  1. Tenant Provisioning: Create subscriber tenant profile, upload company logo, and configure Entra ID / Local administrator accounts.
  2. Regional Hub Configuration: Register operating facilities (e.g. Atlanta Metro Hub), set local timezones, and define operating hours / company holidays.
  3. Register Stations & Mobile Crews: Add CNC sawjet machines and field mobile trucks as assignees with unique schedule color codes.
  4. Build Activity Pipeline: Create standard milestones (Laser Template, CAD Match, Sawjet Cut, Install, QA Signoff) with inter-activity lead time buffers.
  5. Load Slab Inventory: Enter initial stone materials, colors, bundle numbers, and barcode IDs (e.g. SLB-ATL-2026-0041) with bin/rack warehouse locations.
  6. Setup Builder Master Hierarchy: Create the first Builder Account (e.g. Lennar GA), Subdivision Community (Austin Park), and Lot (Lot 000036) with ERP External IDs.
  7. Configure Table Matrix & Forms: Build digital form templates, room specification sub-tables, and touch signature fields for field tablets.
  8. Create First Job & Schedule: Create the first work order (e.g. SAP-SO-10170), allocate warehouse slabs, and apply the automated activity schedule template.
  9. Test Shop Floor & Mobile Sign-Off: Simulate shop floor kiosk cut execution on Sawjet Line 1 and mobile PWA offline signature capture.
  10. Generate API Keys & Connect ERP: Generate live REST API credentials (sm_live_...) and verify two-way sync with the Outbound ERP Retry Queue.
